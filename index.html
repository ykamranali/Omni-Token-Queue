From Walk-In to Served in Three Steps

No proprietary hardware required to get started.

Customer takes a token

At a kiosk, on a phone via QR code, or through an online appointment — the customer picks a service and gets a ticket instantly.

Agent calls the next token

From a browser-based terminal, the agent calls, recalls, or transfers the customer between workflow steps in one click.

Display & voice announce

The TV display and voice announcement update automatically — "Token B012, please proceed to Counter 1."

Everything Configurable, Nothing Hardcoded

One platform for any organization — change services, forms, and rules from the dashboard, never the source code.

Dynamic Service Management

Create, edit, enable, or retire services on the fly — icons, colors, duration, priority, and which branches or counters can serve them.

Drag-and-Drop Form Builder

Build customer intake forms from 20+ field types — Emirates ID, passport, signature, file upload, QR code — with validation and visibility rules.

Custom Workflow Builder

Chain steps like Reception → Document Check → Payment → Issuance, with conditional routing and automatic or manual counter assignment.

Smart Queue Rules

Token prefixes, priority levels, VIP handling, max waiting time, no-show policy, recall limits, and emergency queue behavior — all per branch or service.

TV Display & Digital Signage

Now-serving boards, scrolling messages, promotional banners, and emergency alerts — fully brandable layouts and themes.

Voice Announcements

Bilingual (or any language) sound announcements with configurable scripts, TTS voice, speed, volume, and repetition — or upload your own audio.

Real-Time Reporting

Build custom reports from any data field — average wait time, tokens served, no-shows — with charts, KPIs, and scheduled delivery.

Roles & Permissions

SMS, Email & WhatsApp Templates

Editable notification templates with live variables — token number, counter, wait time, appointment date — sent automatically at each step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about running Omni Token Queue.

What is Omni Token Queue?

A fully configurable queue and token management system — kiosk ticketing, agent terminals, TV displays, voice announcements, and reporting — managed entirely from one admin dashboard.

Can I add services and forms without a developer?

Yes. Services, customer intake forms, workflows, and queue rules are all built and edited from the admin dashboard — no code changes needed to onboard a new branch or business type.

Does it support Arabic and other languages?

Yes. Forms, displays, and voice announcements are language-configurable, with full bilingual English/Arabic support out of the box.

Can it integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. Integrations, API keys, and notification channels (SMS, WhatsApp, email) are configured per company, so it can sit alongside your CRM, HIS, or ERP.

What happens if the internet drops at a kiosk?

Kiosk and display clients are designed to keep functioning locally and sync once connectivity returns, so a dropped connection doesn't stop the line from moving.

How many branches can I manage?

The system is multi-tenant and multi-branch by design — manage as many companies, branches, and counters as you need from one dashboard.
